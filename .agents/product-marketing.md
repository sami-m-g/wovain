## Product Overview

**One-liner:** wovain turns a designer's direction into a print-on-demand drop: researched, drawn, priced, listed on Fourthwall and staged as a paused Meta campaign.

**What it does:** You describe the drop: the mood, the references and who it's for. wovain checks what your audience is into this week, drafts graphics in that direction and waits for your edit. Then it lists the approved pieces on your Fourthwall shop, priced to keep a set margin, and writes several ads for a Meta campaign. The campaign is created paused, so nothing spends until you turn it on.

**Product category:** print-on-demand for independent designers. It sits on the same shelf as print-on-demand storefronts (Fourthwall, Printful) and the design and ads tools a label strings together around them.

**Product type:** web app (SaaS), also available as an app inside ChatGPT.

**Business model:** subscription with a small free allowance; see /pricing. Billing is being built (Polar). The merchant runs on their own Fourthwall shop, fal account and Meta ad account, so production and ad spend are billed by those services, not by wovain. Fourthwall stays the merchant of record for product sales.

## Target Audience

**Target customers:** independent fashion designers and small labels (one to five people) who sell, or want to sell, graphic pieces through print-on-demand: tees, sweatshirts, totes, caps and prints.

**Decision-maker:** the designer or founder, who is also the art director, merchandiser and marketer.

**Primary use case:** releasing a small, coherent capsule on print-on-demand without spending a week on listings, pricing math and ad setup.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Turn a creative direction into a few finished, sellable pieces this week.
- Price every piece so it actually makes money after fees.
- Get the drop in front of the right people with ads that don't look like stock ads.

**Use cases:**
- A seasonal capsule (three to six pieces around one theme).
- A collaboration or event drop that needs to go live fast.
- Testing a new direction with a small paid audience before committing to cut-and-sew.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** for a small label, a print-on-demand drop costs a week of non-design work: researching what will land, preparing files for each blank, writing listings, working out prices that survive fees, and building ads.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Print-on-demand storefronts handle printing and shipping but leave research, pricing and ads to you.
- Generic AI image tools make pictures, not products: no blanks, no print sizes, no prices.
- Agencies and freelancers cost more than a small capsule earns.

**What it costs them:** days per drop, underpriced pieces that lose money after card fees and refunds, and ad budgets spent on campaigns set up badly.

**Emotional tension:** fear of putting their name on something generic; worry that an automated tool will spend money or publish without asking.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** print-on-demand storefronts with built-in design tools (Printful, Printify) — they fall short because the designer still does the research, pricing and ads by hand.
**Secondary:** generic AI design tools (image generators, template editors) — they make artwork but stop before a priced, listed product.
**Indirect:** doing it yourself or hiring a freelancer — slow, or too expensive for a small capsule.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- One flow from direction to a listed, priced product and a staged campaign.
- Every step stops for the designer's approval, unless they turn on autopilot.
- Prices come from the real base cost of each Fourthwall blank, with a floor of $10 profit and a 30% margin.
- Campaigns are always created paused.

**How we do it differently:** wovain works inside the merchant's own accounts (Fourthwall, fal, Meta), so the products, art and ads belong to them and are visible where they already work.

**Why that's better:** the designer keeps the taste decisions and gives up only the chores.

**Why customers choose us:** they get a coherent drop live in an afternoon and can see the math behind every price.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "AI will make my label look generic." | You set the direction and approve every piece. Reject with a note and wovain draws another round. Nothing is listed without your yes. |
| "Will it spend my money?" | No. Campaigns are created paused, with a daily budget you choose ($1 to $500). You turn them on in Meta. |
| "Will the prices make sense?" | Every price starts from the blank's real base cost and keeps at least $10 profit and a 30% margin after card fees, sales tax and a refund reserve. |
| "I don't want to hand over my shop." | wovain connects with the official sign-in of each service. You can disconnect at any time. Products are created hidden until you publish them. |

**Anti-persona:** high-volume print-on-demand sellers who want thousands of listings, trademark or fan-art sellers, and anyone looking for cut-and-sew manufacturing today.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** a week of admin per drop; underpriced listings; ads that don't convert.
**Pull:** an afternoon per drop, prices with the math shown, ads staged and ready.
**Habit:** the designer's own process in Illustrator, the Fourthwall dashboard and Ads Manager.
**Anxiety:** losing creative control; surprise spend; a tool publishing without asking.

## Brand Voice

| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Editorial | Short, considered sentences; concrete nouns from the studio (blank, proof, edit, capsule) | Precious, poetic or vague | "You set the direction. wovain does the production." |
| Restrained | One claim per sentence; numbers instead of adjectives; no exclamation marks | Cold or legalistic | "Every price keeps at least $10 profit and a 30% margin." |
| On the designer's side | Taste stays with the designer; wovain handles the chores | Flattery or hype about creativity | "Nothing is listed without your yes." |
| Honest about money | Fees, margins and spend stated plainly | Hedging or fine print | "Campaigns are created paused. Nothing spends until you turn it on." |

**Tone by context:**
- Support and docs: calm, step by step, second person, one task per page.
- Launch: what's new, who it's for, one number.
- Errors: what happened and the next step, in two sentences.
- Sales: one designer writing to another.
- Social: a little looser, still restrained.

**Terms we own:** "a drop", "your direction", "created paused".
**Never use (and why):** "agent", "MCP", model and vendor names on marketing pages (internal detail that makes it read like a demo); "apparel-on-demand" (not the positioning); invented customer counts or results (we have none yet).
