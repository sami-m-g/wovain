## Direction contract

THESIS: every drop ships with its own label. The evidence wovain produces (price math, sources, the judge's verdict, "paused") is set like a garment's hang tag and woven care label: compact, specified, honest. Refuses the AI-gradient SaaS hero, the split hero and the row of icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: calico ground and deep-plum dark mode, plum ink, one cochineal dye rationed to what is live or chosen (primary action, current step, selection), saffron only on the plum rail. (Palette and logo set by the approved brand, 2026-10-25.) Familjen Grotesk display, Geist for UI with tabular figures, Geist Mono only for codes and money lines on a tag. One hairline weight everywhere; selection is the same line drawn heavier. The hang tag (chamfered top, punched eyelet, ruled spec lines) is the one shaped object; everything else is calm rounded product UI.

STORY: the visitor sees the actual approval screen with a real tag hanging off it, understands brief, research, designs, approve, Fourthwall, paused Meta campaign, trusts the numbers because they are the code's own, and writes a brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left-aligned headline and lead over a working brief field with Start a drop; below, full width, the real app window (run tape, a design awaiting approval, Approve and Change), with the hang tag hanging over its right edge carrying price, blank, fees, profit, margin and "campaign paused". Labelled as an example.
