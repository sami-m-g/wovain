## Platform

web

## Users

A solo print-on-demand seller with a Fourthwall shop and a Meta ad account who wants AI to run more of the process. They work in ChatGPT or on the wovain website. Hackathon judges are a secondary audience for the marketing page.

## Product Purpose

wovain turns a seller's brief into a launched product drop: it researches trends, generates designs, waits for the seller's approval, publishes the products on Fourthwall, makes ad variants and builds a paused Meta Advantage+ campaign. An autopilot mode runs the same flow with fewer stops. Success: real Fourthwall products and a ready Meta campaign, while the seller only briefed and approved.

## Positioning

Every step ends in an approval built from real Fourthwall products, placements, prices and margins, and the campaign launches paused, so the seller stays in control of spend.

## Operating Context

- Works in ChatGPT (MCP app) and on the web app.
- Connected services: Fourthwall (catalog, publishing), Meta (ads), fal (images and video).
- Powered by Nebius (NVIDIA Nemotron), Qloo (taste graph), Tavily (trend research), Fourthwall, Meta and GitLab Duo (the build loop).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Seller flow: brief, trend picks, design candidates, approve or give feedback, products, ad variants, launch a paused campaign.
- Autopilot toggle; run history.
- Connections page for Fourthwall, Meta and fal.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: wovain (lowercase).
- Accent: indigo dye #3B4CCA.
- Voice: friendly coach. Warm, explains the why in one sentence, still leads with the numbers.

## Evidence on Hand

- No customers, sales, testimonials or performance data yet. Do not fabricate any. Example data in the app is labelled as a demo.

## Product Principles

1. Approve, don't operate: the seller decides; wovain does the clicking.
2. Show the evidence: prices and margins come with sources or math.
3. Real over mock: use real Fourthwall products, prices and stock.
4. Reuse, don't rebuild: Fourthwall, Meta Advantage+ and the host agent do the heavy lifting.
