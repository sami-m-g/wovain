## Principles

1. **Approve, don't operate.** The designer decides; wovain does the production work.
2. **Show the evidence.** Prices, margins and scores come with sources or math.
3. **Real over mock.** Real Fourthwall products, prices and stock.
4. **Reuse, don't rebuild.** Fourthwall, Meta, Qloo and the model providers do the heavy lifting.
5. **Code enforces limits.** Models decide inside limits that code applies afterward.

## Brand

Name: wovain (lowercase). For independent fashion designers and small labels selling through print-on-demand only. Voice: editorial and restrained. Short, considered sentences, numbers instead of adjectives, on the designer's side and honest about money, for example "Campaigns are created paused. Nothing spends until you turn it on." A new logo is in progress.

## Evidence

There are no customers, sales or testimonials yet, and none are claimed anywhere in these docs.
