---
title: Meta Ads
description: Wovain creates several ad variants per product and a campaign that stays paused.
---

Meta is where the ads run.

- **Ad variants.** For each product, wovain creates several concepts, each with a hook, an audience, Feed and Stories visuals, text variants and a call to action.
- **Audience.** The targeting suggestion comes from [Qloo](/docs/integrations/qloo).
- **Campaign.** One campaign is built with every approved variant.
- **Paused by default.** Every object is created paused and no procedure activates one. The seller starts the campaign in Meta after reviewing it.
- **Connection.** Each seller connects their own ad account.
