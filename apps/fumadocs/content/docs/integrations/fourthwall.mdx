---
title: Fourthwall
description: Wovain designs for Fourthwall's real catalog and creates products in the seller's shop.
---

Fourthwall is the print-on-demand platform and shop.

- **Catalog.** Designs start from real blanks, placements, sizes and base costs, so price, profit and margin are real numbers.
- **Products.** Approved designs become Fourthwall products. Autopilot creates them hidden and makes them public only when the seller chose live publishing and every limit passed.
- **Connection.** Each seller connects their own shop; wovain acts through that connection.

Fourthwall's MCP server is the upstream for these calls. Image upload uses the upload-URL route; the media-upload-link route is not available to wovain.
