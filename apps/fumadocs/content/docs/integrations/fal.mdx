title: fal
description: fal generates the artwork and the ad visuals.
---

fal runs the image models.

- **Art.** Print-ready artwork for the chosen blank and placement.
- **Ad visuals.** Feed and Stories images made from the product mockup. Short video is optional and off by default in autopilot because it is the most expensive step.
- **Cost control.** Autopilot tracks fal spend and does not start a step that would cross the run's maximum spend.
