title: Tavily

---

Tavily is called at **runtime**, inside the seller flow, not only during development.

## What it does

- **Moment.** For each Qloo theme, Tavily searches the past week's news and returns sources. This is the "why now" line shown on an idea.
- **Saturation.** Tavily checks how many matching listings exist on marketplaces such as Etsy, Redbubble and Amazon. Few matches score higher.
- **Trademark screen.** Autopilot searches the title phrase and any words in the art before an idea may continue.

Results are marked untrusted when they reach a model, and each idea keeps its source links so the seller can check the evidence.

## Configuration

```bash
TAVILY_API_KEY=...
```

Without the key, the trend step still works from Qloo alone and says that the moment signal is unavailable.
