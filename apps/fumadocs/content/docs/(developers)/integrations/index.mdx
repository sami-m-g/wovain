---
title: Integrations
description: The providers wovain calls and what each one does in the flow.
---

| Provider | Role in wovain | Key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Nebius Token Factory](/docs/integrations/nebius-nemotron) | Hosts NVIDIA Nemotron, which judges designs and makes autopilot decisions | `NEBIUS_API_KEY` |
| [Qloo](/docs/integrations/qloo) | Taste graph: who buys, what else they love, where they are | `QLOO_API_KEY` |
| [Tavily](/docs/integrations/tavily) | Runtime web search: why now, and how crowded a theme is | `TAVILY_API_KEY` |
| [Fourthwall](/docs/integrations/fourthwall) | Catalog, products, prices and the shop | OAuth per seller |
| [Meta Ads](/docs/integrations/meta-ads) | Ad variants and the paused campaign | OAuth per seller |
| [fal](/docs/integrations/fal) | Print art | The merchant's own fal account (Connections) |

Every provider is optional at start-up. A missing key makes that step answer "not configured" with one line on how to enable it, instead of failing the run.
