wovain is also an app in ChatGPT. Ask for a drop in plain words; ChatGPT shows the ideas and designs and asks you before it lists a product or builds a campaign.

## Add wovain to ChatGPT

1. In ChatGPT's settings, turn on developer mode.
2. Add a custom connector with this URL: `https://mcp.wovain.com/api/mcp`.
3. Sign in to wovain when ChatGPT asks, and allow access.

## Run a drop

Ask in your own words, for example:

> Make a spring drop of washed botanical line drawings for my label.

ChatGPT walks through the same steps as the web app: ideas, designs, your approval, the products and a paused campaign. If an account isn't connected yet, it gives you a link to connect it.

Drops you start in ChatGPT appear under **Runs** on the web, and the other way round.
