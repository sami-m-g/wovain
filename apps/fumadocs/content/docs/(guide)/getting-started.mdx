After you sign up, wovain opens one guided flow that takes you to a running campaign. A checklist on the side shows what's done. A timer counts toward 10 minutes and stops when your first campaign is created. You can skip any step and come back to it later.

## 1. Sign in

Go to [wovain.com](https://wovain.com) and choose **Create your account**. Sign up with your email or with Google. With email, open the link we send you to confirm your address: it signs you in. This step is ticked as soon as you arrive.

## 2. Pick what to sell

wovain suggests a few directions for this week. Each one shows why it's rising with an audience right now and how crowded it already is. Pick one and wovain writes the brief.

Want the directions to fit your label? Open **Add your brand** and set your references, voice and colours first.

## 3. Set up your store

Connect your Fourthwall shop, then work through the store cards: payouts, shipping, your site going live and the rest. Each card opens the right page and ticks off when you come back. See [Connecting your accounts](/docs/connecting-accounts).

## 4. Connect Meta and set your limits

Connect your Meta ad account. Then choose how much wovain does on its own, and the most it may spend on ads in a day and a month. Nothing spends without your approval, and never beyond these limits.

## 5. Approve a design

Choose **Find ideas and draw designs**. wovain researches your direction, keeps the strongest ideas and draws designs for them. Open your drop, approve the designs you'd put your name on, and come back: the step ticks off on its own.

## 6. Launch your ads

Your products are listed on Fourthwall. In your drop, review the ad and its daily budget, then start it.

## Here's your campaign

When the campaign is created, the timer stops and **Here's your campaign** opens. It shows your products, a link to your store, your ads with their controls, your spend and limits, and live results, all on one page.

[Creating a drop](/docs/creating-a-drop) walks through the drop flow in detail.
