Your first drop takes three things: a wovain account, your connected accounts and a brief.

## 1. Create your account

Go to [wovain.com](https://wovain.com) and choose **Create your account**. Sign up with your email or with Google. With email, open the link we send you to confirm your address: it signs you in.

## 2. Connect your accounts

Open **Connections** in the app and connect:

- **Fourthwall**, your shop, where the products are listed.
- **fal**, where the graphics are drawn.
- **Meta**, your ad account, where the campaign is built.

You can write a brief and see the first drafts before you connect anything. You need Fourthwall to list products and Meta to build the campaign. See [Connecting your accounts](/docs/connecting-accounts).

## Your brand, if you want one

A brand is optional. In **Get set up**, the brand step can suggest one from what people love, or you can choose **Skip, just help me sell**. With a brand, suggestions stay within your style and each drop starts from your voice and colours; you can change them on any drop.

## 3. Write the brief

Choose **New drop** and describe the drop the way you'd brief a studio. One sentence is enough:

> Washed botanical line drawings for a spring capsule, for people who garden on balconies.

Mention the mood, a reference or two, and who it's for. More detail gets you closer on the first round.

## 4. Approve, list and stage

wovain shows its ideas, then the drafts. Approve the ones you'd put your name on, list them on Fourthwall, and set a daily budget for the campaign. The campaign is created paused; you turn it on in Meta Ads Manager when you're ready.

[Creating a drop](/docs/creating-a-drop) walks through each step.
