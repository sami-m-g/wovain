## Who bills what

A drop involves four bills, each from the service that does the work:

| What | Billed by | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| wovain | wovain | Your plan, see [pricing](https://wovain.com/pricing) |
| Production and shipping | Fourthwall | Paid from each sale; nothing up front |
| Artwork | fal | Per image, to your fal account |
| Ads | Meta | Only after you turn a campaign on, at your daily budget |

Fourthwall stays the seller of record for your products and pays out your profit. Before you publish, each product card shows that profit per sale and the base cost it comes from.

## Credits come back

Research costs credits when it starts. If it finds no ideas, wovain gives those credits back, the same as when a step fails. Starting a drop from a researched idea on Home costs the same research credits, once: that idea was already researched. They come back if the drop can't start.

## Video ads

When a product goes live, wovain makes two short video ads from its mockup: 5 seconds each, one tall (9:16) for Reels and Stories and one square (1:1) for the feed. Each clip costs 10 credits, charged once even if the step is retried. If fal refuses a clip, its credits come back. Either way, a clip that fails never holds up the campaign: it launches with your image ads.

## Promo codes

A promo code adds free credits to your account. Open **Billing**, enter the code under **Redeem a code** and select **Redeem**. A free code asks for no card, and its credits arrive within a few seconds. They never expire.

Each account can redeem a code once, and a code stops working after its end date or once it has been fully redeemed.
