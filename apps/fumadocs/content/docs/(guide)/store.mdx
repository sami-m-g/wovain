**Store** is where you run your Fourthwall shop from wovain: what it needs before it can sell, what sold, what waits for you, and every change to your products. Nothing changes in your shop until you approve it.

## Store setup

Before a shop can take orders, Fourthwall needs a few things only you can give it. The **Get your shop ready to sell** checklist at the top of the page lists them, in the order that unblocks a sale first:

- **Shop connected**, as its owner or a manager.
- **Payouts**, so the money from each sale reaches you.
- **Shop live**. Choose **Go live** and wovain takes a coming-soon shop live once you confirm.
- **Meta pixel**, so your ads learn from the people who buy. No pixel yet? Choose **Set up my pixel**: wovain creates one, connects it to your ad account and sends Meta a test event. Then wovain shows your pixel ID with a copy button and the Fourthwall page to paste it in, since Fourthwall has no way for wovain to paste it for you. The step ticks off when Meta sees visits from your shop.
- **Custom domain**, optional: your shop sells on its Fourthwall address without one.
- **Policies**, to review in Fourthwall.

There is no shipping step: Fourthwall makes and ships your products and sets their shipping options itself.

Each open step has one action: wovain does it, or a link opens the right Fourthwall page in a new tab. Come back to wovain when you're done; the checklist ticks the step off by itself.

## Product pages that sell

A shopper who arrives from an ad decides in seconds. Open a product's menu and choose **Rewrite product page**. wovain writes the page the way an e-commerce copywriter would:

- a title with the main search words first, short enough that search results show all of it,
- one or two lines on who it's for,
- three or four benefits, one per line,
- what your shop can promise: printed to order, and the shipping time Fourthwall gives.

You see the page now and the new page side by side, as a buyer sees them. wovain never invents a fabric, a review or a guarantee. Choose **Use this page** to apply it, or **Write another** for a new take. You can undo it from **Approvals**.

## Products, orders and promotions

- **Products**: price, margin and units sold for each product. Publish, hide, retire, change a price or order a sample.
- **Orders**: refund or cancel, with what it costs you shown before you confirm.
- **Promotions**: discount codes for the products you pick, and gift cards. wovain refuses a discount that would make a sale lose money.
- **Approvals**: everything waiting for your yes, and what was done.
