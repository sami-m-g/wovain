wovain works inside your own accounts. The products, artwork and ads it makes live there, under your name, and you can disconnect any account at any time from **Connections**.

Each account connects with that service's own sign-in. wovain never sees your passwords.

## Fourthwall

Fourthwall is your shop. It prints each order, ships it and runs checkout.

1. In **Connections**, choose **Connect Fourthwall**.
2. Sign in to Fourthwall and allow access to your shop.

wovain reads the catalog of blanks and their base costs, creates products from the designs you approve, and publishes them when you say so. New products start **hidden**, so nothing appears in your shop until you publish it.

Once your shop is connected, **Store** lists what it still needs before it can sell and ticks each step off as you finish it. See [Your store](/docs/store).

## fal

fal draws the graphics for each idea, at print resolution, on your fal account.

1. In **Connections**, choose **Connect fal**.
2. Follow the steps to link your fal account.

Image generation is billed by fal to your account. See [Billing](/docs/billing).

## Meta

Meta is where your ads run. wovain needs an ad account and a Facebook Page.

1. In **Connections**, choose **Connect Meta**.
2. Sign in with Facebook and allow access to the ad account and Page you want to use.

Then open **Store**. Under **Get your Meta account ready for ads**, wovain checks your account and shows a card for each step left: an ad account, a payment method, a Facebook Page, an Instagram account (optional) and the safety rules. Each card says what to do and opens the exact Meta screen in a new tab. Come back to wovain when you're done: the card ticks off by itself.

If the safety rules card asks you to, choose **Reconnect Meta** and allow everything Meta asks for. You can also reconnect or disconnect Meta from **Connections** at any time.

wovain writes several ads for each product and builds a campaign with them. Every campaign is created **paused**, with the daily budget you set ($1 to $500). Spend starts only when you turn it on in Meta Ads Manager.

## Disconnecting

Choose **Disconnect** next to any account in **Connections**. What wovain already created stays in that account: products stay in your shop, and campaigns stay in Ads Manager.
