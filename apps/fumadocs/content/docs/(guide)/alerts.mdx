wovain tells you when something happens to your money or needs you. Open **Settings > Alerts** from the menu under your name.

## What you hear about

| Alert | When |
| --- | --- |
| Ads paused or stopped | A rule paused an ad or a campaign, or stopped every campaign at a cap or your loss limit. |
| Budget raise requests | A product sells and a rule asks to raise its budget. |
| Approvals waiting | Another action waits for your yes or no. |
| First sale | Your shop makes its first sale. |
| Failures | A job failed after several tries. |
| Suggestions and finished runs | New ideas from autopilot, and runs that finish. |

You get each alert once on each channel, even when wovain retries.

## Where alerts go

- **Email**: to the address on your account.
- **Phone**: sign in to the wovain app and allow notifications.
- **Telegram**: choose **Connect Telegram**. Telegram opens wovain's bot. Press **Start**, then come back and choose **I've pressed Start**. The link works for 15 minutes. To stop, choose **Disconnect** or block the bot.

Tick the channels you want for each alert, then choose **Save**. Money alerts go to every channel until you change them; suggestions go to your phone only.

## Quiet hours

Turn on **Hold phone and Telegram alerts at night** and set the hours. During quiet hours, phone and Telegram alerts wait until the end. Email still goes at once. The hours use your browser's time zone.
