A drop is one release: your brief, the ideas, the designs you approve, the products on Fourthwall and a Meta campaign. Start one from **New drop**. Every drop is saved under **Runs**, so you can come back to it.

## Start from what sells now

You don't need an idea, or a brand, to start. **Home** opens with three to five things to sell now, under **What to sell now**. Each one is backed by proof that it sells:

- **Shops advertising it**: how many shops run Meta ads for it, and how many have kept them running for 14 or 30 days. Shops rarely keep paying for ads that don't sell. This needs your Meta account connected.
- **What sells on the marketplaces**: bestsellers and reviews on Etsy, Amazon and Redbubble.
- **How crowded it is**: how many near-identical listings already exist.

A theme with no proof isn't suggested, and neither is one that is too crowded: wovain weighs what sells (ads kept running, bestsellers, reviews) against how many similar listings and advertisers already compete for it, and looks for the sweet spot in between. Ideas with thin evidence rank below proven ones, so you may see fewer than five. Every card has the same four parts: what to make, why it sells, who buys and where, and the competition. A part wovain couldn't read says why. Each suggestion names the design, who wears it and why they buy it, with the ads and listings linked. Choose **Who buys this** to see their age, interests, top cities and the fashion brands they already wear, from Qloo's taste data. On the Home the ideas show one at a time: use the arrows or the arrow keys, or swipe on a phone.

With a brand, the suggestions stay within it; turn on **Ignore my brand this time** to see everything. Without one, **Show me others** brings a new set. Choose **Start this drop** on a suggestion: the drop opens at its designs with that idea and its evidence, so the brief and the trends are already done. Check its tone and colours while its research report loads, then choose **Make designs**: the designs use all of it, and their credits are taken then. It costs the research credits once, like the research for a written brief. Evidence older than six hours is read again first. Its research dossier (why now, how crowded, the price band and the risks) starts at the same time, with no extra click or credits, and shows on the drop page.

## One page per drop

In the new app layout, rolling out now, each drop has its own page, read top to bottom: why it sells, who buys and where, then the ideas, designs, product, campaign and results. Each section shows whether it is done, needs you, is working or hasn't started. The step that needs you opens in its own section, and **Review** reopens a finished one.

A **Decided by Qloo** mark sits next to the decisions Qloo makes: why the idea sells, who buys it and the campaign's targeting. Open it to see what Qloo decided and the evidence: taste affinity, news this week, similar listings, audiences, places and Meta interests.

## 1. The brief

Describe the drop in a sentence or a short paragraph: the mood, a reference or two, and who it's for.

> Washed botanical line drawings for a spring capsule, for people who garden on balconies.

Under the box, a few starters from what sells now fill it in for you to edit. They never start the drop: **Research trends** does. **Not sure? Pick a researched idea** takes you back to the ideas on the Home.

Name at least one thing your audience loves, such as a band, film, brand or place. wovain starts from what that audience likes. If it can't place your brief, the run stops and shows examples. Choose **Write a new brief** and name one.

## 2. The ideas

wovain comes back with a few ideas for the drop. Each one is scored on three things:

- **Taste**: how closely it matches what your audience already likes.
- **This week**: whether people are talking about it now.
- **Competition**: how many similar listings are already out there.

Each idea shows its sources. Pick the ones you want to see drawn.

In the new app layout, every idea is the same card with the same four sections, in the same order: **What to make**, **Why it sells** (with its proof), **Who buys and where**, and **Competition**. A section without data stays on the card and says why, for example "Not available: not read yet". The title is a readable name for the product. The Qloo tag shows as evidence, and the **Decided by Qloo** mark opens what Qloo decided.

## 3. The designs

wovain drafts the graphics for each idea and checks them for print: size, legibility at chest size, number of colours, and names or marks you shouldn't use. Each design shows that check as **Ready** or **Needs work**, with what to fix.

- **Approve** the designs you'd put your name on.
- **Reject** one with a note, such as "heavier type, lose the leaves", and choose **Make another** for a new round on the same idea.

Nothing moves on without at least one approved design.

## 4. The products

Approved designs become products on real Fourthwall blanks. Each price starts from the blank's base cost and keeps at least **$10 profit** and a **30% margin**, after card fees, sales tax and a refund reserve, rounded up to a whole dollar.

Products are created **hidden**. Choose **Publish to Fourthwall** when you're ready for them to appear in your shop. One confirm shows the product, its price and your shop; confirming is your approval, so it goes live right away. Once every product is live, the drop moves on to its campaign. You can change any price later in Fourthwall.

## 5. The campaign

The Campaign step shows the ad creatives, the copy, Qloo's audience, the budget and the spend rules, with one **Launch campaign** button. Without Meta connected it shows the creatives and **Connect Meta to launch**, which brings you back here. Set a daily budget between $1 and $500. wovain writes several ads for each product, each from a different angle, and builds a Meta campaign with an ad per variant.

When the drop has two to five designs, wovain tests them together in one campaign. Each design gets its own share of the daily budget, so every design gets a fair chance, and Meta moves the rest of the budget to the designs that sell. Designs that lose money are paused, and the winner can be scaled. Each campaign card shows every design's spend, clicks, add-to-carts, sales and profit, and whether it is testing, paused (and why) or the winner. More than five designs make one test per five.

The ads reach the people who love what your brand loves: Qloo picks that audience from the brands in your profile, and the ads are written for them. If wovain can't find that audience, it makes no ads and tells you why. Add a brand you love to your profile, or try again in a minute if Qloo didn't answer.

The campaign is created **paused**. Review it in Meta Ads Manager and turn it on when you're ready to spend.
