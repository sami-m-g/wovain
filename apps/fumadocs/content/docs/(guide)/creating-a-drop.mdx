title: Creating a drop

A drop is one release: your brief, the ideas, the designs you approve, the products on Fourthwall and a Meta campaign. Start one from **New drop**. Every drop is saved under **Runs**, so you can come back to it.

## 1. The brief

Describe the drop in a sentence or a short paragraph: the mood, a reference or two, and who it's for.

> Washed botanical line drawings for a spring capsule, for people who garden on balconies.

Name at least one thing your audience loves, such as a band, film, brand or place. wovain starts from what that audience likes. If it can't place your brief, the run stops and shows examples. Choose **Write a new brief** and name one.

## 2. The ideas

wovain comes back with a few ideas for the drop. Each one is scored on three things:

- **Taste**: how closely it matches what your audience already likes.
- **This week**: whether people are talking about it now.
- **Competition**: how many similar listings are already out there.

Each idea shows its sources. Pick the ones you want to see drawn.

## 3. The designs

wovain drafts the graphics for each idea and checks them for print: size, legibility at chest size, number of colours, and names or marks you shouldn't use. Each design shows that check as **Ready** or **Needs work**, with what to fix.

- **Approve** the designs you'd put your name on.
- **Reject** one with a note, such as "heavier type, lose the leaves", and choose **Make another** for a new round on the same idea.

Nothing moves on without at least one approved design.

## 4. The products

Approved designs become products on real Fourthwall blanks. Each price starts from the blank's base cost and keeps at least **$10 profit** and a **30% margin**, after card fees, sales tax and a refund reserve, rounded up to a whole dollar.

Products are created **hidden**. Choose **Publish to Fourthwall** when you're ready for them to appear in your shop. You can change any price later in Fourthwall.

## 5. The campaign

Set a daily budget between $1 and $500. wovain writes several ads for each product, each from a different angle, and builds a Meta campaign with an ad per variant.

The ads reach the people who love what your brand loves: Qloo picks that audience from the brands in your profile, and the ads are written for them. If wovain can't find that audience, it makes no ads and tells you why. Add a brand you love to your profile, or try again in a minute if Qloo didn't answer.

The campaign is created **paused**. Review it in Meta Ads Manager and turn it on when you're ready to spend.
