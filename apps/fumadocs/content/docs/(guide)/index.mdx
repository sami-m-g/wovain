wovain is print-on-demand for independent designers and small labels. You describe a drop: the mood, the references and who it's for. wovain does the production work around it.

1. It looks at what your audience is into and what is moving this week.
2. It drafts graphics for each idea, sized and checked for print.
3. You edit: approve the pieces you'd sign, or send one back with a note.
4. It lists the approved pieces on your Fourthwall shop, priced from each blank's real base cost.
5. It writes several ads and builds a Meta campaign around them, **paused** until you turn it on.

Every step stops for your approval, unless you turn on [autopilot](/docs/autopilot). Nothing spends money by itself.
