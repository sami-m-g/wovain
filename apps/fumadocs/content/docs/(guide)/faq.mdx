## Who is wovain for?

Independent designers and small labels who sell graphic pieces through print-on-demand: tees, sweatshirts, totes and prints on Fourthwall.

## Will it make my label look generic?

You set the direction and approve every piece. Reject a design with a note and wovain draws another round. Nothing is listed without your yes.

## Will wovain spend money on ads by itself?

No. Every campaign is created paused, at the daily budget you choose, from $1 to $500. Spend starts only when you turn it on in Meta Ads Manager. Autopilot never turns on spend either. Once it runs, wovain pauses ads that lose money and stops spend at your limits. See [Spending safely](/docs/spending-safely).

## How are prices set?

From the blank's real base cost on Fourthwall: at least $10 profit and a 30% margin, after card fees, sales tax and a refund reserve, rounded up to a whole dollar. To change a price, use **Change price** on the Store: it shows your profit and margin in every size as you type, against what Fourthwall charges to make it, and refuses a price that loses money.

## What does it cost?

wovain's plans are on the [pricing page](https://wovain.com/pricing). Production is paid from each sale through Fourthwall, artwork is billed by fal and ad spend by Meta, each to your own account. See [Billing](/docs/billing).

## Who owns the designs?

You own your briefs and the designs you approve. wovain screens drafts for names and trademarks, but you stay responsible for what you publish.

## Can I use my own artwork?

Not yet. Today wovain drafts the graphics from your brief, and you shape them with feedback until they're right.

## What happens if I disconnect an account?

wovain stops acting in it. What it already made stays there: products stay in your Fourthwall shop and campaigns stay in Meta Ads Manager.

## Can I use wovain in another language?

Yes. The app and these docs are in English, French and Spanish.

## Where do I get help?

Write to [support@wovain.com](mailto:support@wovain.com).
