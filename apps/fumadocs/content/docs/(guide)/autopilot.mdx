With autopilot on, wovain runs the whole drop without stopping for you. It picks the strongest idea, approves a design only when its print check says it's ready, lists the product and builds the campaign.

## Turn it on

In **New drop**, switch on **Autopilot** before you start. Write the brief and set the daily budget, then choose **Run on autopilot**.

## What stays the same

Autopilot follows the same rules as a drop you run yourself:

- A design that needs work is never approved.
- Every price keeps at least $10 profit and a 30% margin.
- The daily budget stays between $1 and $500.
- The campaign is created **paused**. Autopilot never turns on spend.

## Checking what it did

Each autopilot drop is saved under **Runs**, with the decision it made at each step and why. If a step can't finish, for example because an account isn't connected, the run stops there and shows what to fix.

## Who has it

Autopilot comes with the Studio and Label plans, which aren't on sale yet. Until then it is on for wovain's team, and for every plan on the preview site, next.wovain.com.

## Suggestions (preview)

On the preview site, autopilot also watches for you. Every 6 hours it asks Qloo which brands are rising with your audience and searches this week's news for them and for your niches. When the two line up, it suggests a drop under **Autopilot**, with the sources it used.

What happens next depends on your autonomy level:

- **Suggest only**: the suggestion waits for you. Choose **Start the drop** or **Dismiss**.
- **Draft for me**: autopilot starts the run, then waits for you to pick the ideas.
- **Publish, ads paused**: autopilot runs the drop and publishes it. The campaign stays paused.

Autopilot spends at most your plan's daily autopilot credits on the drops it starts. The **Autopilot** page lists each step it took and why.
