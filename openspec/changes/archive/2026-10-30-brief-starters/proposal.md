# Proposal

Issue: https://github.com/sami-m-g/wovain/issues/286

## Why

The brief step showed the same five full idea cards as the Home, with the brief box four to six screens below them. A card click there started a run that researched the idea again. One place to pick a researched idea (the Home) and one to write a brief (the brief step) gives each screen one job.

## What Changes

- The brief step drops the direction cards. The brief box comes first, then 3 to 4 starters from the kept directions (each direction's title; its brief fills the box), then "Not sure? Pick a researched idea", a link to the Home's directions, and one primary "Research trends".
- A starter only fills the box. The path that started a run from a direction in the brief step is removed.
- Fixed example starters (en, es, fr) show when no directions are kept.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `drop-directions`: the directions live on the Home; the brief step shows starters.
