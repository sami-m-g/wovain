## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Home ideas one at a time

The Home's "What sells now" SHALL show one idea card at a time, with previous and next buttons, the keyboard's left and right arrows and a swipe on touch screens to move between them. It SHALL show the position ("2 of 5") and announce it politely to screen readers, label the carousel and each slide, and change slides without gliding when the merchant prefers reduced motion.

#### Scenario: Move to the next idea

- **WHEN** the merchant presses "Next idea" or the right arrow key on the Home's ideas
- **THEN** the next idea's card shows and the position reads "2 of 5"
